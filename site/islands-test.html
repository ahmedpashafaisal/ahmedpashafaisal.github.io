Key

  1. Island sign = category. Island size follows its number of games. Click the sign: the car drives to that island.
  2. Cabin = game. Name always visible. Click: the car drives there and the card opens.
  3. Landmark = featured game. Wildlings, Magic Mineral, Storm Alert.
  4. Card. Screenshot, one line, role, "Open game" (straight to the game page), store links.
  5. Ford. Dotted line in the water: the car splashes through.
  6. Empty plot. Bulb beat, decided later.
  7. Start. The car waits here. Scroll stays normal: the map scrolls with the page.
