Thesis

Designing humour into a political board game about media censorship: Twist the News.

BSc Electrical and Electronic Engineering

  • KUET, Bangladesh
  • 2014

Master's thesis

Integrating Humor Into the Design of a Political Game About Media Censorship

Ahmed Pasha Faisal

  • MSc Digital Learning Games
  • Tallinn University
  • 2024

Supervisors: Dr Mikhail Fiadotau, Dr Anastasiya Fiadotava

Abstract

Serious games rarely use humour, and few guidelines exist for games that joke about politics. I designed Twist the News, a card-based party game about media censorship, and studied how its humour shaped play.

1. Questions

How to design a political game that incorporates humor to address media censorship?

How does incorporating humor impact player engagement and understanding of a serious message in a political game?

2. The game

Players are news editors under an authoritarian regime. A rotating Propaganda Minister reveals news that would embarrass the regime. Editors use propaganda cards to spin it into headlines that praise it.

The players write the jokes, not the designer.

3. Method

Design-based research. Twelve politically aware Bangladeshis, aged 18 to 35, played online in groups of four. Each session ended with a group interview.

Thematic analysis of the recordings and interviews.

4. Findings

  • Players made the humour. The funniest moments came when the cards limited their options.
  • Humour raised engagement and discussion.
  • For two players it failed: they read the game as teaching propaganda.
  • Four guidelines: playtest early and often, let players create the humour, balance the cards, give a setting players trust.
